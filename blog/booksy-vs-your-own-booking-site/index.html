Booksy vs. Your Own Booking Site: Which Actually Makes You Money?

October 2026 · by Omy Grace · 6 min read

Booksy and similar marketplaces give you one real thing: a booking button with built-in discovery. In exchange, they take a recurring cut, control the relationship, and keep most of the traffic when someone searches your name. Before you renew, it's worth comparing the two honestly.

What you actually rent from a marketplace

  • Discovery: people searching the marketplace may find you.
  • Booking infra: calendar, reminders, payment — so you never build it.
  • A branded page — branded to them, not to you.

That's real value. It's also a recurring fee in addition to any payment-processing percentage, and it stays on your books forever, whether or not it brings you a single new client.

The math that decides it

Take the marketplace's monthly fee and ask one question: does it bring me at least one new regular client every month? A new regular at a $70 ticket visiting monthly is worth roughly $840 over a year. If the marketplace delivers that client, it pays for itself. If most of your bookings come from your own name and Google, you're paying rent on traffic you already own.

Most shops end up wanting both worlds: a booking page on your domain — connected to the calendar you already like — plus reviews and reminders that work for you, not the platform.

A note on tools you may see advertised

You'll see YouTube ads pitching "AI tools that run your entire salon." Treat them the same way as any subscription: does it replace a cost you already have, or add a new one?
